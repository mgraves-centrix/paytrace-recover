# Sample walkthrough visual brief

Audience and task: a first-time buyer, merchant, or judge should understand how one fictional paid-but-locked-out case reaches a verified outcome.

Feeling: precise, human, reassuring, forward-moving. Retain the PayTrace navy and blue, existing logo, and the product's plain-language safety boundaries. The new reference is an interactive scenario workbench: a compact story header, persistent journey rail, framed case record, and a bottom action dock. Adapt that composition to PayTrace rather than imitating the reference product's colors or screenshots. The chapter headline and Maya's narrative beat lead each moment; the payment → access → lesson signal remains supporting proof inside the case record. Keep buyer perspective and merchant control adjacent to the record; put scripted specialist detail behind one disclosure.

The primary interaction is the next moment. The route supports deep links with `?step=`, previous/next buttons, a desktop journey rail, and a native mobile moment selector. The action dock stays available while the case record scrolls in the document. Motion is a brief state arrival, with a reduced-motion fallback. The fictional label remains visible in the introduction, case record, boundary note, and completed state; this route does not represent connected acceptance.

Each moment carries one short human narrative beat above the framed evidence. It explains Maya's expectation, the merchant's decision, or why a technical success is not yet a customer success. The primary button must stay legible on hover, focus, and press; its blue variant overrides the shared secondary-button hover surface. On narrow screens the rail becomes a labeled select, while the premise, current chapter, story, record, and next action remain in reading order.

Review scope: `dist/demo.html`, `dist/demo.css`, and `dist/demo-journey.mjs`. The visual change does not alter the sample state machine or any real payment, approval, grant, or verification path.
