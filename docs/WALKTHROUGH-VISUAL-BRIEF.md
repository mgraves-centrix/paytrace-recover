# Sample walkthrough visual brief

Audience and task: a first-time buyer, merchant, or judge should understand how one fictional paid-but-locked-out case reaches a verified outcome.

Feeling: precise, human, reassuring, forward-moving. Retain the PayTrace navy and blue, existing logo, and the product's plain-language safety boundaries. Open with Maya's broken promise: a receipt above a still-locked lesson, rendered as a compact editorial scene. This is a story premise, not a live payment claim. The chapter headline and Maya's narrative beat lead each moment; the dark payment → access → lesson signal board is supporting proof. Keep buyer perspective and merchant control adjacent to the current moment; put scripted specialist detail behind one disclosure.

The primary interaction is the next moment. The route supports deep links with `?step=`, previous/next buttons, and direct moment selection. Motion is a brief state arrival, with a reduced-motion fallback. The fictional label remains visible in the hero, boundary note, and completed state; this route does not represent connected acceptance.

Each moment carries one short human narrative beat above the action and evidence. It explains Maya's expectation, the merchant's decision, or why a technical success is not yet a customer success. The primary button must stay legible on hover, focus, and press; its blue variant overrides the shared secondary-button hover surface. On narrow screens the decorative scene drops out, leaving the premise, current chapter, and story in reading order.

Review scope: `dist/demo.html`, `dist/demo.css`, and `dist/demo-journey.mjs`. The visual change does not alter the sample state machine or any real payment, approval, grant, or verification path.
