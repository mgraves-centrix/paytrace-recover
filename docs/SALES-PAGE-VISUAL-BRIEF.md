# PayTrace sales page visual brief

Audience and task: Merchants and judges should grasp the paid-but-locked-out problem, inspect a fictional sample, and enter the merchant workspace.

Feeling: assured, precise, human.

Direction: Preserve PayTrace navy, electric blue, white, Manrope, and DM Sans. Turn the homepage sample case into a spatial scene: a completed receipt at left, a bright locked lesson at right, and a visibly interrupted handoff between them. Maya's short question grounds the diagram in the learner's experience. The amber gap is a warning accent, not a success state.

Hierarchy: A direct editorial headline and one primary sample-case action lead. The receipt and lesson are the two focal objects; the broken connector explains their relationship at a glance. A quieter merchant-workspace link follows the primary action.

Desktop and mobile: The headline and illustration sit side by side on desktop. The overlapping objects preserve independent readable text and the course title is never hidden. On mobile, the headline and actions lead, then the illustration becomes a vertical receipt → gap → course → learner sequence. The rest of the sales page keeps its existing system.

Motion: A one-shot arrival gives the scene depth. The evidence panel responds to a deliberate record switch. No looping animation or delayed interaction. Reduced-motion users see the complete static frame.

Constraints: Use HTML and CSS with existing assets and fonts; no animation library or new provider claims. Keep the sample explicitly fictional and the connected acceptance gap visible elsewhere on the page.
