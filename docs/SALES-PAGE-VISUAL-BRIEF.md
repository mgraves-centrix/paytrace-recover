# PayTrace sales page visual brief

Audience and task: Merchants and judges should grasp the paid-but-locked-out problem, inspect a fictional sample, and enter the merchant workspace.

Feeling: assured, precise, human.

Direction: Preserve PayTrace navy, electric blue, white, Manrope, and DM Sans. Replace the layered course-card hero with a single case signal: a completed payment, a visible handoff gap, and missing course access that requires review. The course artwork becomes a compact part of the access record rather than a second dominant card.

Hierarchy: A direct editorial headline and one primary sample-case action lead. The evidence frame gives payment, access, learner context, and next decision distinct roles. A quieter merchant-workspace link follows the primary action.

Desktop and mobile: The headline and case signal sit side by side on desktop. On mobile, the headline and actions lead; the case signal follows as a complete, readable sequence. The rest of the sales page keeps its existing system.

Motion: A one-shot arrival and connector draw clarify the handoff. The evidence panel responds to a deliberate record switch. No looping animation or delayed interaction. Reduced-motion users see the complete static frame.

Constraints: Use HTML and CSS with existing assets and fonts; no animation library or new provider claims. Keep the sample explicitly fictional and the connected acceptance gap visible elsewhere on the page.
