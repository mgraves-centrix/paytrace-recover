# Sandbox checkout visual brief

- Audience and task: a merchant running one PayPal sandbox purchase to open a recovery case.
- Feeling: focused, trustworthy, calm. Preserve PayTrace blue, the existing wordmark, and the sample workshop identity.
- Composition: a short introduction leads directly to a prominent checkout card; a quieter side panel explains the payment-to-case boundary. The operator key is the first visible task when the session is locked.
- Typography and color: readable product UI scale, dark blue headings, white working surface, blue action, and a separate green sandbox label. Avoid using decorative color as proof of payment or access.
- Visual: the workshop cover is a compact CSS product tile, not a fabricated screenshot. The three outcome steps are text-first and remain secondary to the form.
- Responsive: stack the checkout before context on mobile; keep form controls full width and preserve the purchase price and sandbox status.
- Motion and assets: short button state transition only, removed for reduced motion; no new external assets or dependencies.
- Truth boundary: the purchase is sandbox only. Checkout opens a case after verified capture and never implies that course access was granted.
