# Sandbox checkout visual brief

- Audience and task: a merchant running one PayPal sandbox purchase to open a recovery case.
- Feeling: focused, assured, distinctive. Preserve PayTrace blue, the existing wordmark, and the sample workshop identity.
- Composition: a dark purchase-to-case signal stage sits beside a bright, task-focused checkout. The operator key is the first visible task when the session is locked. This replaces the former pale two-card dashboard layout.
- Typography and color: a large editorial statement on the dark stage, readable product UI scale in the checkout, white work surface, blue action, and a separate green sandbox label. Decorative color does not imply payment or access success.
- Visual: the workshop cover is a compact CSS product tile. Two larger signal cards show the waiting payment and the case that will open after capture, without fabricating a completed outcome.
- Responsive: put checkout first and the narrative stage second on mobile; keep form controls full width and preserve the purchase price and sandbox status.
- Motion and assets: short button state transition only, removed for reduced motion; no new external assets or dependencies.
- Truth boundary: the purchase is sandbox only. Checkout opens a case after verified capture and never implies that course access was granted.
